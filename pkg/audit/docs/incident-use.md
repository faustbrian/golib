# Incident use

Authorize a bounded tenant scope and time window outside the library. Export in
stable cursor order to a restricted incident workspace, verify chain or Merkle
anchors before analysis, and record who requested and accessed privileged
fields. Correlate audit records with logs and traces by identifiers without
merging their retention or trust models.

Preserve original exports, query parameters, counts, cursors, digests,
checkpoints, key IDs, tool versions, and access decisions. A failed integrity
check is evidence of inconsistency, not proof of a specific attacker. Do not
rewrite or silently skip invalid records; isolate the range and follow the
documented repair boundary.
