## BoxPacker common-process comparison

The separately recorded comparison used PHP 8.5.8, Go 1.26.6, BoxPacker
4.2.0, one warm-up, and ten alternating fresh processes per implementation.

| Implementation | Wall p50 | Wall p95 | Solve p50 | Solve p95 | Peak RSS | Quality |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| BoxPacker | 35.707 ms | 54.313 ms | 2.375 ms | 2.765 ms | 48,709,632 B | 1 box, 2 items, verified |
| knapsack | 3.997 ms | 19.625 ms | 154.959 us | 286.667 us | 5,767,168 B | 1 box, 2 items, verified |

Wall timing includes process startup, runtime initialization, autoload,
fixture setup, solving, and serialization. It excludes adapter compilation,
Composer installation, and independent verification. Both delayed processes
stopped within 51.8 ms under a 50 ms deadline. Comparable allocation counts
are unavailable across the PHP and Go process boundary, so none are reported.
These measurements describe one tiny common subset and are not a general
speed claim. Complete samples and disclosures are in
`raw/2026-08-15-boxpacker-runtime.json`.
