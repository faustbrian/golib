# WSDL 1.1 decision index

The authoritative decision records moved to
[`docs/specification-decisions.md`](../../docs/specification-decisions.md)
without changing `WSDL11-DEC-001` through `WSDL11-DEC-003`. That canonical
register also adds `WSDL11-DEC-004` and the cross-version `WSDL-DEC-*` series.

This compatibility pointer prevents two copies of the same accepted decisions
from drifting. Historical links to this path remain valid.
